<h2 id="about"><img src="assets/label-01.svg" height="30" alt="01 — ABOUT" /></h2>

I'm Hardik, based in Stuttgart, Germany, and I'm working my way into AI engineering the most direct way I know: **by shipping**.

Every couple of weeks I build one small AI tool that solves a real problem, for job seekers, students, or anyone facing German paperwork. Then I write down exactly how it works. Most of them run on local models through Ollama, so they're free to use and run on your own machine. My Master's thesis designs **Steward**, a multi-agent system that keeps a team's knowledge up to date, with a human approving every change.

<h2 id="thesis"><img src="assets/label-03.svg" height="30" alt="03 — THESIS" /></h2>

**Intelligent Knowledge Management in Collaborative Environments**<br/>
<sub>Master's thesis · Oct 2026 → Apr 2027</sub>

> **Research question:** How can Agentic AI support human-in-the-loop knowledge management to maintain accurate, context-aware, and continuously evolving knowledge in collaborative environments?

<img src="assets/project-02.svg" alt="02 — Master's thesis: Intelligent Knowledge Management in Collaborative Environments. AI agents spot outdated team knowledge in chat and tickets, and a human approves every change. Currently in the data collection phase, October 2026 to April 2027." width="100%" />

In teams, decisions are made in chat and tickets, while the documents that people and AI chatbots read slowly go out of date. My thesis designs **Steward**, a multi-agent system that spots outdated knowledge and suggests the update, *like a pull request, but for team knowledge*. A human approves every change before it is saved.
